Only the first data set has a fitting trajectory.
Perturbation are introduced at the torque level, with 30ms duration, and a magnitude cmd of 10N.
Ball impact are introduced at the torque level, with 30ms duration.
The experiments were conducted over several days.
The target height for the ball was 1.7m, and the kinematic coefficient for the simulated environment was 6.
The z null position of the simulated environment is given by the following equation: z_virt = (z_meas - z0)*kin_coeff. z0 = 0.32m.
The coefficient of restitution was 0.6, and the gravity 9.81.

No incident were reported during those experiments.
