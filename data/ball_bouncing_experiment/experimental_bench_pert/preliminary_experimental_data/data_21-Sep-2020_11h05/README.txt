Experimental trial without perturbation with hand manipulation during admittance controlled interractions (PI gain : Kp = 0.015, Ki = 0.08).

This test should help to find a relation between force and movement when no perturbation occurs.

2 tests were conducted:
- with step like inputs (movement to a position then back)
- with sine like inputs (smoother movement), with 2 different frequency (1st 100bpm, 2nd 70bpm (2/4)).
