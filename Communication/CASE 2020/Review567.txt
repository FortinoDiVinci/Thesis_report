Reviewer 4 of CASE 2020 submission 271

Comments to the author
======================

This paper presents an experimental robotic setup dedicated
to human arm endpoint impedance measurement in a cyclic
task with physical interactions. The ball bouncing
benchmark task, classical in human movement science, is
selected here for its simplicity, yet representativeness of
hand-eye synchronization and intermittent physical
interactions. The paper is well organized and clearly
written. The study design is sound and preliminary
experimental results were reported and analyzed. It
demonstrated the feasibility of using interactive robotics
for human impedance estimation. In addition, future reseach
avenues to enhance the work are identified.

1.	What are the major difference and the major
findings of this work comparing to the previous work
conducted by the authors? 
2.	In the experimental design, could the readers
provide more information on the study design and the choice
of ranges of parameters. 
3.	Discuss the potential application of this work. The
bio-mimetic rhythmic control design was mentioned as one
area of application. What is the challenge of expanding
this work out of the experimental environment? 
4.	The explanation of Figure 5 can be strengthened.
